## Why this exists

Most of our clients do not have a strategy problem. They have a sequencing problem. The investments are obvious in isolation; the question is which one first, and what it does to the three after it.

We sit between the machine vendors and the management team. Vendors optimize for a sale this quarter. Internal teams optimize for the line they already run. Our job is to hold the longer view.

## What you can expect

A typical engagement runs six to twelve weeks. We work in tight loops with your finance lead, your production manager, and at least one operator who has been on the floor longer than the current ERP system. The output is rarely a thick deck. More often it is a one-page decision memo, a model your CFO can stress-test, and a sequenced plan with the assumptions written in the margin.

We do not stay longer than we need to. When the decision is made and the project handover is clean, we step back.
