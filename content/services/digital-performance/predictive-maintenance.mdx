## What predictive actually predicts

A useful predictive maintenance system does not tell you a bearing will fail at 14:32 on Thursday. It tells you a bearing has degraded enough to schedule replacement at the next planned stop, three weeks out, and gives you the confidence interval. That is what your maintenance team needs to plan around.

We are skeptical of any predictive system that offers more precision than that without a long track record on your specific equipment.

## Start small

We always start with one or two assets. The cost of getting predictive maintenance wrong on a critical asset is high, and the only way to know it is right on your equipment is to run it for a quarter. Once a small instrumented set is producing trustworthy predictions, scaling is straightforward.
