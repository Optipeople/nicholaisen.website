## Why we are useful here

Most of our clients have a deep view of their own segment and a thinner view of the segments next door. We sit across the segments. We have watched panel producers move into doors, kitchen producers move into bathrooms, solid wood producers move into engineered products. The mistakes are remarkably consistent. Underestimating tooling complexity. Overestimating sales-channel transferability. Misreading the working capital implications of a longer lead time.

We bring those patterns to the table early so you do not pay the tuition again.

## What we will not do

We will not run your sales process. We will not pretend to be your strategy department. We will not stay on the venture forever. The job is to help you make better, faster decisions in the first eighteen months, then step back as the venture either becomes a normal part of your business or gets closed cleanly.
