## The most expensive surprise

The most expensive surprise in industrial production is the capacity surprise. The new customer the sales team won six months ago is now in production, the lead times have crept out, the existing customers are unhappy, and overtime is running at twenty percent of base hours.

It is preventable. Most capacity surprises are visible in the data three to nine months before they hit. The problem is rarely the data; it is the absence of the standing meeting where someone is paid to look at it.

## Why partnership

Capacity planning works best as a long-running relationship rather than a project. The model has to evolve as your business does, the meeting cadence has to outlive the original participants, and the value compounds over time. That is why this sits inside our partnership offer rather than as a one-off engagement.
