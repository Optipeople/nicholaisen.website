## The shape of the work

Operational collaboration looks different in different businesses. For a 60-person family-owned producer, it might mean a monthly day on site, a weekly call with the production manager, and a quarterly board attendance. For a multi-site group, it might mean rotating site visits and a standing seat on the operations committee.

What stays constant is the seniority of the consultant assigned, the continuity of that person over the contract, and the deliberate restraint about what we attend. Showing up to every meeting is not the goal. Showing up to the right ones is.

## When it ends

Sometimes a partnership ends because the business has built the internal capability we were filling in for. That is the best outcome. We have closed several partnerships that way and stayed in informal contact afterwards. The willingness to end cleanly is what makes the relationship trustable in the first place.
