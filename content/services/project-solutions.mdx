## What turnkey actually means

There is a version of turnkey where a vendor delivers a line that meets the spec on paper and walks away. We have inherited a few of those. The plant runs at sixty percent of nameplate, the operators have built workarounds, and nobody is quite sure who owns the documentation.

We mean something different. A turnkey project from us closes with a line that runs the planned product mix at the planned cadence, with operators who can defend the way it works and a maintenance team that can fix it without phoning the supplier. If we have not delivered that, the project is not finished.

## Scale

We have led projects from a single integrated drilling cell to entire production facilities producing more than a million units a year. The discipline is the same. The size of the calendar changes.
