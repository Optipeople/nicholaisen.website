## Why this model exists

Many of our clients found us for a single project and then asked, quietly, whether we could just keep showing up. The work that follows a successful project is often the most valuable work of all, and it does not fit neatly into a fixed-scope engagement.

A partnership is the answer to that. We commit to a defined rhythm, a defined senior contact, and a defined set of decision rights. You get continuity of judgment without paying for continuity of headcount.

## What it is not

A partnership is not a retainer for tasks we have not yet defined. It is not a way to lock in a vendor relationship. If we are not adding senior judgment that you would otherwise have to hire for, we should not be on a partnership contract. We will tell you so.
