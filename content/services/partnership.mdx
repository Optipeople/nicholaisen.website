## Why this model exists

Most partnerships start as a project. The project works, and the client asks if we can stay involved. What follows a successful project is often more valuable than the project itself — but it does not fit a fixed scope or a one-off fee.

A partnership gives it a proper framework: a defined cadence, a named senior contact, and clear responsibilities. You keep the continuity; the terms stay transparent.

## What it is not

A partnership is not a retainer for work we have not defined. If we cannot point to a specific production question, a capacity decision, or a business development opportunity where our involvement earns its place, we should not be on a partnership contract. We say this at every annual review — and we mean it.
