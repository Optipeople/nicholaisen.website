## Where the time goes

In our experience, the average wood or panel producer running two shifts has somewhere between fifteen and thirty percent of theoretical capacity hiding in plain sight. It hides in the gap between scheduled and actual changeovers. It hides in the buffer that grew without anyone deciding it should. It hides in the operator workaround that became permanent five years ago.

Production optimization is the discipline of finding those minutes and engineering them out, line by line, week by week.

## How we work

We start on the floor, not in the conference room. The first week is structured observation: we measure what is actually happening before proposing what should change. The ideas that hold tend to come from the people running the equipment every day. Our job is to give those ideas a structure, a measurement, and a clear path to implementation.
