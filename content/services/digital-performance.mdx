## Honest data first

A surprising number of dashboards in our industry lie politely. They average over the wrong window, they treat planned downtime as if it were free, and they let the loudest event of the day drive the headline number. Operators stop trusting the screen, and within a quarter the screen is wallpaper.

Opti is built on the opposite principle. The first job of the platform is to be believable. If a number on the dashboard does not match what an operator saw, we want to know about it the same shift.

## What changes

When the data is honest, decisions get faster. Standups shorten because everyone is reading the same chart. Maintenance moves from reactive to scheduled. Planners stop padding lead times to compensate for unknowns. The shop floor starts proposing improvements instead of waiting for them.
