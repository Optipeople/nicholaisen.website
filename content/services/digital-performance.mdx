## From machine to factory

Many companies start with one box at one machine. It is simple to get going — but it shows only one corner of the factory, and it is hard to see the full picture across lines and shifts.

Nicholaisen and OptiPeople built Opticloud to solve the next step: not just one machine, but the whole factory. Machines, lines, shifts, orders, energy and operators — unified in one data layer.

The difference is not technical. It is strategic. If you only see one machine, the value is priced as one machine. If you see the factory's data layer, the whole conversation changes.

## What it solves

Three types of pressure we are most often called in for:

**Maintenance:** Unplanned stoppages and a maintenance approach that is reactive rather than preventive. Opticloud gives you visibility over downtime cost, response time and planned versus unplanned work — so you can move from reacting to preventing.

**Quality and process:** Manual registrations, inconsistent quality and weak traceability across orders and processes. Opticloud links order and process context, making defects, rework and waste traceable and reducible.

**Operations and investment:** Scattered data and a weak basis for prioritisation. Opticloud provides energy per unit, cost-to-produce and data quality — a clear basis for the decisions that actually move things.
