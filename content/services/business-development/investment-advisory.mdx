## How we are paid

Our fee is fixed and disclosed up front. We do not accept referral fees, kickbacks, or commission from any machine builder, integrator, or financier. If a vendor offers, we tell you.

This matters more than it sounds. Most capex decisions in our industry are made with at least one party in the room whose income depends on a particular answer. We do not want to be that party.

## What a good business case looks like

A good business case is short. It states what is being bought, what it will produce, what it will cost across ten years, and what has to be true for the numbers to hold. It includes one section the team would rather not write, the section on what could go wrong. The strongest cases we have built always include that section.
