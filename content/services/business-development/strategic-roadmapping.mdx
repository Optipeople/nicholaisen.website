## Why sequence matters more than selection

We have rarely seen an industrial business pick the wrong machine. We have often seen them pick the right machine at the wrong time. Buying the new flow line before resolving the bottleneck upstream. Adding capacity before the sales team is ready to fill it. Hiring the operators before the building extension is finished.

Roadmapping is the discipline of putting the right work in the right order, then defending that order against the noise of the quarter.

## What we hand over

A plan that fits on one page, a model that backs every line on the page, and a quarterly review template your team can run without us. We expect to be invited back to the first one or two reviews. After that, the plan should belong to you.
