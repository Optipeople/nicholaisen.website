## When this helps

Framing workshops earn their keep when the business knows something needs to change but cannot agree on what. The symptoms are familiar. Long meetings that loop. A capex list that grew without ever being prioritized. A new opportunity that nobody quite has time to assess.

Two days of structured work usually unlocks the next six months of execution.

## What we expect from you

Six to eight people in the room, including at least one owner-level decision maker and at least one operator who has been with the business for a long time. Phones on the table, face down. The workshop only works when the people who need to decide are actually in attendance.
