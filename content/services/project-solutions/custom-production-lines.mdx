## Why custom

Standard lines exist for a reason. When the product mix is narrow and the volumes are predictable, an off-the-shelf line is the right answer.

Custom is the right answer when your product mix is genuinely different. A wider thickness range than the standard model handles. A material with awkward dust characteristics. A downstream packaging operation that drives an unusual takt time. A footprint constraint that forces an L-shape instead of a straight line. In those cases, a properly engineered custom line will outperform a standard line by a wide margin.

## How we choose vendors

We do not have a preferred vendor list. We have a working knowledge of the credible builders in each category and a habit of revisiting that knowledge every project. The right vendor for your line depends on the spec, the lead time, the service network in your region, and how the vendor's engineering team gets on with yours. We assess all of it before recommending.
