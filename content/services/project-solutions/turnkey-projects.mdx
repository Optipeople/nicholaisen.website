## What you sign up for

A turnkey contract with us is short. The detailed scope sits in the appendices, and the appendices are written in plain language. The acceptance criteria are quantitative. There is one number for the date the plant accepts its first commercial order, one number for the throughput it sustains over the first thirty days, and one number for the OEE it holds over the first ninety. We sign up to all three.

## What we ask of you

A small steering group with the authority to make decisions. A nominated operations owner from your business who will run the plant after handover and who is involved from day one. And the willingness to push back on us when our recommendations do not fit your context. The best turnkey projects we have delivered are the ones where the client team challenged the design hard, early.
