## Automate the right operation

Most failed automation projects we have inherited have one thing in common. They automated the wrong operation. The visible operation. The one a visiting executive would point at.

The right operation to automate is usually less visible. It is the one that drives the changeover time, or the one that injures backs over a five-year window, or the one that introduces variability the downstream process cannot absorb. We help you find it before the integrator's quote arrives.

## Where humans still win

We are not on a mission to replace operators. We are on a mission to take the work that is dangerous, repetitive, or beyond human consistency, and engineer it out. The work that requires judgment, pattern recognition, and the kind of feel that takes years to develop, stays with the people. That is the bargain we recommend, and it is the bargain that produces sustainable lines.
