## The investment you do not have to make

Capacity improvement work routinely defers capex by twelve to twenty-four months. Sometimes it removes the need entirely. We have walked into projects where the brief was to specify a second production line and walked out three months later having lifted the existing line by twenty-two percent, with the second-line project paused.

This does not always happen. Sometimes the demand is genuinely beyond what the existing assets can deliver, and the right answer is to invest. But the question is worth asking properly before signing the order.

## What capacity is not

Capacity is not maximum theoretical throughput. It is the rate you can run sustainably, with acceptable quality, with the team you actually have, on the products you actually sell. We measure capacity that way because that is the capacity that pays the bills.
