## Why we built our own platform

We did not set out to build a software product. For most of our history, when a client needed production insight, we deployed one of the established platforms and configured it to their plant. The platforms are competent. We still recommend them in some cases.

What we kept running into was a gap between what the platforms promised on the surface and what they delivered when the operators actually had to use them. The dashboards were designed for the management report, not the shift hand-over. The downtime classification was so granular that operators picked the first option and moved on. The integration to the older machines on the floor was a project in itself, often costing more than the platform license.

After about the fifteenth project where we were apologizing for the same shortcomings, we started building our own. Opti is the result.

## What Opti does

Opti is a production insight platform built for the shop floor first. It connects to your machines, captures availability, performance and quality in real time, and presents the data in interfaces designed for operators wearing gloves in dusty environments.

<Callout title="What is in the box" tone="info">
Live OEE per machine, per shift, per product. Operator-friendly downtime classification, configurable in under thirty seconds. Pre-built shift, daily, weekly and monthly reports. Predictive maintenance signals on rotating, hydraulic and pneumatic systems. Integration to ERP, MES, quality and maintenance systems. A weekly improvement-review template that turns the data into action.
</Callout>

The platform is in production at multiple sites across Scandinavia and Northern Europe. Most installations go live within two weeks of contract signature.

## What Opti does not do

Opti does not try to replace your ERP, your MES or your CMMS. It connects to them and amplifies them. We have spent enough time inside those systems to know that the boundary between insight and transactional systems should be respected, not bulldozed.

Opti does not promise to predict every failure on every machine. It surfaces the failures it can predict reliably, with confidence intervals, and tells you when it cannot. We are skeptical of platforms that promise more.

Opti does not require a six-month implementation project. If a vendor is quoting a six-month implementation for production insight, the project will run for nine months and the operators will not be using the platform at the end of it.

## How we sell it

We sell Opti as part of an outcome. The license fee is included in the digital performance engagement, and the engagement is sold against a defined OEE improvement target over the first twelve months. If we do not hit the target, we restructure the fee. We are willing to take that risk because we have measured the typical lift across our installations and we know what to expect.

<PullQuote attribution="A panel plant manager, four months after Opti go-live">

</PullQuote>

## Where to start

If you are considering production insight, start with one line and a six-week pilot. Instrument honestly, run a tight weekly improvement meeting, and measure the result against an honest baseline. If the pilot produces measurable improvement and the operators are using the screen voluntarily, scale to the next line. If it does not, you have spent six weeks and learned something useful about either the platform, the line or the cadence. That is a fair price for clarity.

We are happy to talk about whether Opti is the right answer for your plant. We are equally happy to tell you if it is not.
