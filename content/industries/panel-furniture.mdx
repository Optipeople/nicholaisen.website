## A segment under pressure

Panel and furniture producers in Northern Europe are under structural pressure from two sides. Customers expect mass customization at series prices. Material and energy costs have moved permanently higher. The lines that worked in 2019 are not always the lines that work now.

We help producers face that pressure with concrete operational moves rather than slogans. Specific changeover gains. Specific yield improvements. Specific automation decisions framed against specific business cases.

## Where we have made the difference

The largest single improvements we have delivered in this segment come from three areas. First, edgebanding cell optimization, where small flow and changeover changes routinely add ten to twenty percent throughput. Second, nesting yield, where a careful look at part rotation and remnant management often recovers one to two percent of material cost. Third, the integration between CNC, drilling and assembly, where the right buffer and pull discipline removes hours of WIP from the floor.

## On six-sided drilling

We have helped a number of producers think through the six-sided drilling investment. It is one of the most consequential capex decisions in the segment, and it is often made on optimistic throughput assumptions. We have written separately on what a robust business case looks like.
