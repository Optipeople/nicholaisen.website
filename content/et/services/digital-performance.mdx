## Who it is for

Three types of pressure we are most often called in for:

**Maintenance** — Unplanned stoppages and a maintenance approach that is reactive rather than preventive. Visible result: downtime cost, response time and planned versus unplanned work.

**Quality and process** — Manual registrations, inconsistent quality and weak traceability across orders and processes. Visible result: defects, rework, waste and the cost of missing process context.

**Operations and investment** — Scattered data and a weak basis for prioritisation and investment decisions. Visible result: energy per unit, cost-to-produce, data quality and investment consequences.
